# Fighter Edge V2: plan

## Context

Phase 0 is merged on `main` (74a0e61): CI launches the release APK, failed saves show a notice, and tabs keep their state. The app still looks flat and "made by code", and it behaves like separate tools.

The owner wants a second version that:

- looks premium and Apple-like, with more saturated colour and glass;
- is smoother and easier to use;
- has the AI removed for now;
- is a **paid app with a 7-day free trial** that solves one clear pain point;
- keeps the round timer only as an optional tool;
- has a weekly view that does a job;
- has Reaction drill timings that are fixed and justified.

### Owner decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Target user | **Amateur fighters with a bout coming**: camp structure, training on chosen days, eating for training, making weight safely. A fight date is optional, and a "no fight booked" path still gets a full plan. |
| Business model | **Hard paywall.** A 7-day free trial through the **stores' own trials** (Google Play and App Store, via RevenueCat) on both platforms. After the trial, or after cancelling, the account becomes **read-only**: data stays saved and viewable. |
| Paywall placement | Right **after the plan reveal**: sign up → onboarding → the user sees their real week and fuel target → "Start your free week" → app. This is the entry gate, not an upsell. |
| Design process | **Mockups first**, approved by the owner, then the Flutter build. |
| Glass level | **Balanced.** |

### Already approved and still in force

- Deterministic plan engine. Session-template content needs owner or coach sign-off.
- The weekly review only recommends; the user accepts.
- Rest days never punish the user.
- Keep the dark/crimson combat identity. Gold is for Pro/paid only.
- Keep the branded names Camp and Corner. Navigation stays Home, Train, Fuel, Profile.

**Approving this plan also approves:**

- The nutrition model in `docs/NUTRITION_COMPETITOR_RESEARCH.md` §10–14:
  - training-aware calories
  - weight change set as a % of body weight per week
  - training-day / rest-day carb shift
  - a recommend-only weekly check-in
- The domain and template structure in `docs/TRAINING_PLAN_PROPOSAL.md` §2–7.

Template **content** and the open questions in §9 remain owner decisions (listed at the end).

## Phase 2: Reaction drill timing (lane B, 1 PR)

### Problems found (`lib/training/reaction/reaction_drill.dart` L234–322)

- Easier levels sometimes give less time than harder ones.
- Wrestling allows about 1 s for rolls and cartwheels.
- Every move is treated the same.
- Advanced+ striking (90 s) is shorter than Advanced (150 s).
- Jitter of ±100 ms makes the rhythm metronomic.
- Speech time is never measured.
- The 6 s voice cut-off can overlap the next call.

### Evidence-based model (pure `ReactionTiming` class)

```
gap = speed(level) × (Σ combo-cost + finish-cost) + jittered[ margin(level) + recall(level) × (moves − 1) ]
gap ≥ speed(level) × (Σ combo-cost + finish-cost) + 300 ms        (never shorter than the movement itself)
```

- The gap starts at the TTS completion callback, which is measured, not estimated.
- **Only the reaction part is jittered**, never the movement time.
- The jitter distribution is skewed toward shorter gaps, with a 12% chance of an extra 1–2 s. This keeps calls unpredictable, which the evidence supports (fixed gaps let athletes anticipate).

**Level settings**

| Level | Margin | Speed | Recall/extra move | Jitter | Moves/call | Round structure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Beginner | 1500 ms | 1.3 | 300 | ±25% | 1–2 | 1 min work / 1 min rest |
| Intermediate | 1100 | 1.15 | 200 | ±25% | 1–3 | 2 / 1 |
| Advanced | 800 | 1.0 | 150 | ±30% | 2–4 | 3 / 1 |
| Advanced+ | 600 | 0.9 | 100 | ±35% | 2–5 | 3 / 1 |

- Margins sit above a choice reaction time of about 300 ms plus about 200 ms to hear and decide.
- Rounds follow amateur norms: novices 3×2 min, elites 3×3 min.
- Number of rounds: grappling drills 2 rounds; striking and MMA 3 rounds.
- Grappling adds a block rest: +3 s every 3–4 calls on Beginner, +2.5 s every 4–5 on Intermediate, +2 s every 5–6 on Advanced, none on Advanced+.

**Combo cost** (what a move adds inside a call)

| Group | Costs (ms) |
|---|---|
| Punches | jab 300, cross 400, hook/uppercut 450 |
| Kicks | teep 800, low kick 900, mid kick 1100, high kick 1250 |
| Movement and defence | slip/step 400, pivot 500, level change 600, kick check 600, down block/jump 700 |
| Grappling | sprawl 1300, shoot 1800, front roll/cartwheel 2500, back roll 3000 |

**Finish cost** (return to guard, once per call): +300 ms if the call ends on a punch or footwork move, +500 ms if it ends on a kick.

Sources: Muay Thai roundhouse full cycle 1.02 s; jab/cross about 357 ms; hook about 477 ms. Grappling costs are coaching estimates. **[coach sign-off]**

**Worked examples**

| Call | Beginner | Advanced+ | Today |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front roll | ≈ 5.4 s | ≈ 3.3 s | ≈ 1 s |
| Single jab | ≈ 2.3 s | ≈ 1.1 s | — |

### Voice and drill-end handling

- `say()` completion is the measured speech time.
- The fixed 6 s `_maxLine` in `tts_coach_voice.dart` becomes 2 × (syllables × 190 ms + 250) + 1 s.
- A call that cannot finish before the round ends is not started.
- After a pause, resume uses a 2 s countdown instead of the full one.

### Tests

Rewrite `test/unit/training/reaction_drill_test.dart` and `test/flow/reaction_drill_conformance_test.dart`. They must prove:

- The nominal gap strictly falls as the level rises, for every move and for seeded combos.
- Duration, moves per call and jitter never shrink as the level rises.
- The floor clamp always holds.
- No call overruns its round.

Also update the drill-picker stats (`reaction_drill_picker.dart:217`) so they show the real per-level pace.

### 3.3 Client access model

`lib/billing/subscription.dart` gets `AccessState`:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `trial(endsAt)` | in the store trial |
| `active` | paid |
| `readOnly` | trial ended or never started |
| `unknown` | use the last cached state |

- This replaces the per-feature `_proOnly` gate. Everything is included in the paid app.
- Add an optional `freeTrial` field to `BillingProduct` (`lib/billing/billing_gateway.dart`), read from RevenueCat's intro price or the Play free phase in `revenuecat_billing_gateway.dart`. The paywall never claims a trial the store doesn't actually offer.
- One `requireWrite()` choke point in `AppState` and the controllers.
- A `GatedAction` wrapper opens the paywall sheet instead of running a locked action.
- **Read-only must keep working:**
  - history, plan and weight views
  - settings and sign-out
  - account deletion and consent withdrawal
  - Restore
  - the fight-week safety text

### 3.4 Email-verification conflict

`VerifiedAction.purchasePro` currently blocks buying until the email is verified. That would block the paywall right after onboarding.

Change: starting the store trial is allowed once the verification email has been sent; Google and Apple accounts already count as verified. The trial then nudges verification. Receipts are tied to the store account and the uid, not the email.

### 3.5 Paywall and trial experience

- what you get: your camp week, session mode, fuel for training days, the weekly review, safe weight path, drills, recipes;

- monthly and yearly tiles;
- Restore and Terms/Privacy links.

Removals:

- `PaywallTrigger.planReady` as an upsell. It becomes the onboarding gate.

**Trial experience:**

- A "Trial · N days left" chip on Home.
- A local reminder 2 days before the trial ends.

**Telemetry:** `trial_started`, `trial_converted`, `paywall_viewed` (with trigger).

## Phase 6: Visual foundation (lane F, 3 PRs, after mockup approval)

### 6.1 Tokens

- New colour ramps plus glass tint, rim and inner-light tokens.
- Contrast tests (`test/unit/color_contrast_test.dart`, `test/accessibility/visible_contrast_roles_test.dart`) check text over tinted glass **on the brightest glow** and over the solid fallback.

### 6.2 Glass primitives

- `lib/widgets/glass_surface.dart`: `ClipRRect` + `BackdropFilter.grouped` + `ImageFilterConfig.blur(bounded: true)`, sigma about 16, with tint and rim. These APIs exist in Flutter 3.47.2. One `BackdropGroup` sits at `HomeShell`.
- `GlassCard`: the tint-only look, no blur.
- `GlassPolicy` with three levels: full / tinted (no blur) / solid. It drops a level when any of these apply:
  - the new "Reduce transparency" setting is on;
  - `MediaQuery.highContrast` is on. Flutter exposes no reduce-transparency flag, so this is the proxy.
  - a frame probe sees p90 raster time above 16 ms in the first 120 frames. The result is saved.
- Budget: at most 2–3 blurs on screen, never inside list items, never animated.
- Phones without Vulkan, or below API 29, fall back to OpenGL. Check them through the probe; no new dependency is needed.

### 6.3 Chrome

- `AppBackground` (`lib/widgets/premium_effects.dart`) paints the static glow once, inside a `RepaintBoundary`.
- Glass `AppBottomNav` (`lib/widgets/bottom_nav.dart`), with `extendBody` and list bottom padding.
