2. What we process, why, and on what legal basis

2.1 Your account

Email address, display name, password (processed by Firebase Authentication; we never see your password in plain text), whether your email is verified, and when the account was created. If you sign in with Google (or Apple), we receive your email address and name from that provider. A unique account ID is created for you.

Purpose: to create and secure your account and keep your data available across devices. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

2.2 Health, body, and fitness data

If you use these features, we store:

  • Setup and nutrition profile: goal, experience level, training days per week, age, height, current and target weight, body-fat percentage (optional), the energy-equation profile you choose (which corresponds to biological sex), activity level, goal pace, diet type, allergies and intolerances, disliked foods, meals per day, budget and cooking time preferences, and the safety answers you give during setup.
  • Calculated targets: your daily calorie and macronutrient targets.
  • Logs: weight entries; foods you log (name, portion, calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, time); training sessions, timer workouts and reaction drills (date, duration, perceived effort, notes); drill progress.

Some of this is health data (special-category data under Art. 9 GDPR). Purpose: to calculate your targets, show your progress, and give you guidance. Legal basis: your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give before you enter this data. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the data or your account. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before it.

2.3 AI coach (Pro, optional)

If you use the AI Fighter Brief or chat, our server (Google Cloud Functions, USA) sends the following to OpenRouter, Inc. (USA), which forwards it to the AI model provider it selects:

  • your calculated targets and the day's food-log summary,
  • your diet type, allergies, and disliked foods,
  • your chat message and up to eight previous messages in the conversation.

Your name, email address, and account ID are not included. We store a daily usage counter to enforce fair-use limits. We don't store the AI conversation on our servers.

2.4 Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment card details. RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) receives the purchase receipt from the store and tells our server your subscription status. We store your plan, the product, the store, the expiry date, and whether it will renew, linked to your account ID.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We keep billing records where commercial and tax law requires it (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

2.5 Usage analytics (only with your consent)

If you allow it, the app sends anonymous usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase. Examples are "paywall viewed", "onboarding step viewed", and "meal logged" (as a yes/no flag). The events come with device, operating system, and app version information and an app-instance identifier, and Google may derive an approximate location from your IP address. The events never contain your name, email, account ID, food names, or body or nutrition values. Advertising features are disabled.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; § 25(1) TDDDG). The app asks once, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy. Nothing is collected before you decide.

2.6 Crash reports (only with your consent)

If you allow it, Firebase Crashlytics (Google) receives crash and error reports: the technical error, device model, operating system, app version, and an installation identifier. They contain no health data or content you entered. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; § 25(1) TDDDG), which you can change in Settings > Privacy.

2.7 Technical data needed to run the service

When the app talks to our servers, Google (as our hosting provider) necessarily processes your IP address, request metadata, and security logs. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating a secure service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; § 25(2) TDDDG).

2.8 Data that stays on your device

Some data is stored only on your phone and never sent to us: language, units and timer settings, your privacy choices, recently used and saved foods, streak-freeze state, onboarding progress, and scheduled training reminders (notifications are created by your phone). Voice cues in drills and the round timer use your device's speech engine. This data isn't included in phone backups.

2.9 When you contact us

If you email us, we use your message and address to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them when the matter is closed, unless we have to keep them by law.

7. Is providing data required?

An email address is required to create an account. Health and fitness data is optional, but the nutrition and tracking features can't work without it. Analytics, crash reports, and the AI coach are fully optional.

8. Automated calculations

Your calorie and macronutrient targets are calculated automatically from the data you enter. They are guidance only and have no legal or similarly significant effect on you (Art. 22 GDPR doesn't apply). They don't replace advice from a doctor or dietitian.

9. Children

Fighter Edge is not intended for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect data from children under 16.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access rules ensure that only you can read your data. Your subscription status can only be changed by our server, never by the app. AI provider keys stay on our server.

11. Changes

We will update this policy when our processing changes, for example if advertising is ever introduced. The effective date above will change, and we will tell you in the app about significant changes.
