In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Fighter Edge and sign in.

Without the app

Email TODO(owner): privacy contact email from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your login, your profile, and everything stored in your account: nutrition setup and targets, food log, weight entries, training log, AI usage counters, your consent records, and subscription status. We also tell RevenueCat, our subscription service, to delete your customer record and purchase history. Data kept only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.

What is kept

  • A minimal record of each subscription event (event ID, type and date), so the same event is never applied twice. Your account ID is removed from it, so it can no longer be linked to you.
  • Apple's and Google's own purchase records, under their policies.
  • Copies in encrypted server backups until they are overwritten on the backup schedule.

A subscription is not cancelled by deleting your account. Cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
