Ethical Guidelines

Fighter Edge mobile app. Version 1.0, effective TODO(owner): publication date.

How we build Fighter Edge, and what it will never do. These guidelines cover the app, its AI coach and everything we publish. If the app ever falls short of them, tell us (see the end of this page).

1. Safety comes before making weight

  • Weight plans follow the International Society of Sports Nutrition's 2025 position stand on weight cutting in combat sports: 0.5–1 kg a week during camp, and in fight week only food changes (less fibre, fewer carbohydrates).
  • We never plan water cuts, sweating off weight in a sauna or sweat suit, diuretics, laxatives, vomiting, or skipping food. In fight week the app tells you to drink normally.
  • If a weight limit can only be made with a water cut, the app says so and tells you to work with a qualified coach or dietitian. If a limit is not safe by the fight date, the app says so plainly and shows the lightest limit you can make safely.
  • Fighter Edge is for people aged 16 and over. Weight-cut plans are for adults: for anyone under 18, the app makes no plan and points to their coach.
  • Safety information and warnings are never behind the paywall.

2. Numbers you can check

  • Calories, macronutrients and weight paths are calculated by the app, not guessed by AI, using published formulas and position stands. We test those calculations, and the screens that set limits name their source.
  • Every figure is an estimate. Your needs may differ, especially if you have a medical condition.

3. AI that knows its limits

  • The AI coach explains the numbers the app has calculated. It does not set new targets, diagnose, or prescribe.
  • Our server checks every AI answer before you see it. An answer that contains a number the app did not supply, or a dangerous method such as dehydration, diuretics, laxatives, sauna cuts or starving, is discarded and replaced by a safe standard reply.
  • The AI coach is optional and works only with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.
  • AI answers can still be wrong. Use your own judgement, and ask a professional when it matters.
  • Before we change the AI model, we test the coach against realistic questions from athletes, including risky ones.

4. Respect for your body

  • Weight is a number to make on the scale, not a measure of your worth. We don't use before-and-after photos, body shaming or guilt.
  • If you have or have had an eating disorder, talk to a doctor before cutting weight, and consider not using the weight-cut features.

5. Your data

  • We ask for health data only to run the features you use, and only with your explicit consent.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is stored and why.
  • You can delete your account and all its data at any time in the app.

6. Money and advertising

  • Fighter Edge Pro is optional. Prices and renewal terms are shown before you buy, and you can cancel in your App Store or Google Play account.
  • Pro is ad-free. The free app shows no ads today. If we ever add them, we will never advertise diet pills, diuretics, laxatives, "rapid weight loss" products or anything else that promotes unsafe weight cutting, and we will never target ads using your health data.

7. Honest content

  • Recipe photos are real photographs used under open licences, with the photographer credited in the app. We don't use AI-generated food images.
  • We name the sources behind our safety limits.

8. Built for everyone

We test the app with text enlarged up to 200%, with screen readers and with high contrast on small phones. It is available in English and German.

9. Tell us

If something in the app goes against these guidelines, or an AI answer looks unsafe, write to TODO(owner): support email. We read every report and fix what we get wrong.
